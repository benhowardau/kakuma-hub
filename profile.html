My profile

Your profile lives on a key that only you hold, the same idea as your bitcoin. No password, no account with us. Clients find you in the directory and in every Nostr app in the world.

How this works

When you press Create, your phone makes a key and keeps it. Everything you publish is signed by that key, so nobody can change your profile but you. The key has a backup phrase that starts with nsec. Write it down on paper like your bitcoin words: if you lose the phone and the paper, you start again with a new profile.

Your profile is published to public Nostr relays, which are free servers anyone can read. That is why the hub never needs to run a server, and why your profile also appears in apps like Primal, Damus and Amethyst, where bitcoiners can find you and zap you.

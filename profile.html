Why there is no password

When you tap Create, your phone makes a key and keeps it, the same way a bitcoin wallet does. Everything you publish is signed with that key, so only you can change your profile, and there is nothing for the hub to hold, lose or be hacked for.

The key has a backup phrase that starts with nsec. Write it on paper next to your bitcoin words. Lose the phone and the paper, and you simply start a new profile.

Your profile is stored on public relays, free servers that anyone can read, which is why the hub runs no server of its own. The same profile appears in bitcoin apps like Primal, Damus and Amethyst, where people around the world can find you and send you sats. The open standard behind this is called Nostr.

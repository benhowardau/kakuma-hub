My profile

Your phone is your login. Nothing to remember, nothing to write down, and clients anywhere can find you and pay you in bitcoin.

One moment…

How login works

The same fingerprint or face that unlocks your phone unlocks your profile. Underneath, your phone keeps a key and signs everything you publish with it, so only you can change your profile, and the hub holds nothing that can be lost or hacked.

Phones with Google or Apple backup carry that login to your next phone by themselves. On a phone without it, save the backup file once and keep it in your Drive or email. Lose everything, and you make a new profile in five minutes; the hub moves your verified badge at a Saturday class.

Your profile lives on public relays, free servers anyone can read, which is why the hub runs no server. It also appears in bitcoin apps like Primal, where people around the world can find you and send you sats. The open standard behind it is called Nostr.

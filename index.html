What the hub is

Kakuma holds around 300,000 people from South Sudan, Somalia, Congo, Burundi, Ethiopia and beyond. Many work online for clients abroad and get paid in dollars that lose a large cut at every exchange. The hub teaches bitcoin from the ground up: what money is, why bitcoin is different, how to hold it with your own keys, and how to be paid in it from anywhere in seconds.

Classes are free and in person at Clinic 7. The library lends printed books. The directory lets clients find and pay members. The market sells things made here to buyers outside, so new sats keep coming into Kakuma.

What the hub is

Around 300,000 people live in Kakuma, from South Sudan, Somalia, Congo, Burundi, Ethiopia and beyond. Many already work online for the world and lose a large cut of every dollar at the exchange counter.

The hub teaches bitcoin from the ground up and puts it to work: free classes at Clinic 7, a library of printed books, a directory where clients find and pay people here, and a market that brings new sats into Kakuma.

Made in Kakuma

Beadwork, leather, paintings and drums made by people from South Sudan, Somalia, Congo, Burundi and Ethiopia who live here. Buy a piece with bitcoin and the sats go straight to the maker.

How buying works

  1. Message the maker with the Order link and agree the piece, the price in sats and the shipping to your country.
  2. Pay the maker's lightning address from any Lightning wallet. It arrives in seconds.
  3. The maker posts it from Kakuma and sends you the tracking number.

Prices are set by the makers. A circular economy only works if sats come in from outside, so every order here is new money into Kakuma, not money going round in a circle.

Sell your work here

Add what you make from your profile: a name, a price in sats, two sentences and a photo link. It appears here with your lightning address. Or bring one to the hub on a Saturday and we photograph and list it with you.

Learn bitcoin

Pick the path that fits you. Every link is bitcoin only and free. No other coins, no trading, no promises of returns.

Why bitcoin and not crypto

You will meet people offering Dogecoin, Litecoin, new tokens and trading groups. Bitcoin is different in one way that matters: nobody runs it, nobody can print more of it, and nobody can take yours while you hold your own keys. Every other coin has a company or a founder behind it who can change the rules. That is why the hub teaches one thing well.

Start here, everyone

  1. Bitcoin Basics: the hub's own six-lesson course with a certificate. Takes about two hours.
  2. My First Bitcoin: the free diploma course and workbook used by community hubs around the world.
  3. The whitepaper: nine pages from 31 October 2008. Read it once, even the parts you skip.
  4. 21 Lessons: short essays on what bitcoin teaches about money, time and trust.

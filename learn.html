Learn bitcoin

Pick the path that fits you. Everything here is bitcoin only and free.

Why only bitcoin

Nobody runs it, nobody can print more of it, and nobody can take yours while you hold the keys. Every other coin has a company or a founder who can change the rules. So the hub teaches one thing well.

Start herefor everyone
  1. Bitcoin Basics: the hub's own six-lesson course with a certificate. Takes about two hours.
  2. My First Bitcoin: the free diploma course and workbook used by community hubs around the world.
  3. The whitepaper: nine pages from 31 October 2008. Read it once, even the parts you skip.
  4. 21 Lessons: short essays on what bitcoin teaches about money, time and trust.
